Where the money went

Two patterns worth naming. Hong Kong became the venue of choice for the sector's public capital: Momenta's $751M IPO in July 2026, Pony.ai's $860M and WeRide's $308M dual-primary listings in November 2025, Hesai's $535M in September 2025, the largest raise in lidar history, Horizon Robotics' $696M IPO plus $1.4B of placements, and CaoCao's $236M. And Uber appears as an investor in seven of the thirty events, which is not a venture portfolio; it is a supply chain being purchased. NVIDIA appears across Waabi, Wayve and Nuro, in addition to selling all three their compute.

What is missing from this dataset

A funding tracker that publishes its own gaps is more trustworthy than one that pretends completeness. These are carried in the data file itself, as content rather than metadata.

Separately: 49 of the 561 company records carry disclosed lifetime funding totalling $66.9B. The other 511 either raised privately without disclosure, are funded by parents, or are public bodies. Blank is honest.
