■ BEYOND ROADS

Autonomy shipped years ago,
in other operating domains.

The through-line of this page, stated once and then demonstrated six times: autonomy ships first wherever the operating domain is private, bounded and owned by the same party that owns the vehicles. Public roads are the hardest case, not the first one.

Freight and trucking: the nearest neighbour

Highway freight DRIVERLESS SINCE 2024, SCALING 2026

What is autonomous
Class 8 trucks on fixed highway lanes in the US Sun Belt, hub to hub, with humans handling first and last miles.
Commercial record
Aurora passed nearly 440,000 driverless miles by end of June 2026 across 10 routes, with 100% on-time performance claimed and zero Aurora-attributed collisions, and launched second-generation trucks on 22 July 2026: International LT Series, hardware rated for 1M miles at about half the prior build cost, Roush integrating, targeting a 1,000-truck annual run rate. Volvo Autonomous Solutions began factory-built autonomous freight for AVI-SPL and DSV between Dallas and Houston in June 2026.
Who else
Kodiak went public by SPAC in September 2025, the sector's first listing. Waabi raised $1B in January 2026 with Uber milestone capital tied to 25,000 robotaxis. Torc targets series-production driverless Freightliner Cascadias in 2027. Einride completed a ~$333M SPAC in June 2026. Stack AV launched with over $1B from SoftBank.
Who regulates
FMCSA and the states; the same patchwork as robotaxis, applied to 40 tonnes.
What it teaches
Fixed routes shrink the problem, and the economics compare against a real freight rate rather than a subsidised fare: removing a driver saves $70,000 to $90,000 a year against a $300,000 to $450,000 conventional truck cost, which is thinner headroom than robotaxi decks imply.

Delivery robotics: sharing the pavement

Sidewalk and road delivery COMMERCIAL SINCE ~2018

What is autonomous
Cooler-sized sidewalk robots and small road pods carrying food and groceries.
Commercial record
Starship: roughly 2,700 robots across 150+ locations in six countries, 9M-plus deliveries, 19M km, now redeploying 1,200+ robots from US campuses toward grocery and city routes. Serve Robotics hit its 2,000-robot target by December 2025, a twentyfold year-on-year expansion, partnered with Uber Eats and DoorDash across five metros. Coco runs about 1,000 robots including Helsinki.
Who else
Cartken, Avride, and the memory of Amazon Scout, cancelled in 2022, a reminder that scale does not guarantee survival.
Who regulates
Cities, sidewalk by sidewalk, and that is the story: a Chicago petition and aldermanic blockage, a Toronto ban, and disability-access objections framed as a civil-rights issue rather than a niche complaint.
What it teaches
The sidewalk is a shared commons, and fleet size does not confer social licence. Road AV has no equivalent of this governance fight yet; it will.

Mining: the one that got there first

Autonomous haulage COMMERCIAL SINCE 2008

What is autonomous
Haul trucks of several hundred tonnes, plus drills and trains, inside fenced surface mines.
Commercial record
Roughly 3,832 autonomous haul trucks operating globally as of July 2025, up from 2,080 a year earlier, with China leading at 2,090, then Australia, Canada and Chile. The first commercial system was Komatsu's AHS at Codelco's Gabriela Mistral mine in Chile in 2008, starting with four 930E trucks; Caterpillar's MineStar Command followed commercially in 2013 and has now hauled over 5B tonnes autonomously, claiming roughly 20 percent lower operating cost, 30 percent higher productivity and 50 percent fewer accidents.
Who operates
CHN Energy leads by fleet, then Guanghui Energy, BHP, Rio Tinto, Yankuang, Fortescue and Suncor; Rio Tinto's Pilbara grew from 130 trucks in 2020 to over 250 by 2026. Chinese OEMs Tonly, LGMG, EACON, Waytous, CIDI and XCMG are scaling fast, and BHP, Rio Tinto and Fortescue have all signed with XCMG.
Who regulates
Mine-safety authorities, inside private property, which is most of the answer.
What it teaches
Eighteen years of commercial operation wherever the domain is private, bounded and owned by the vehicle owner. This is the thesis of the whole page, proven at 400 tonnes.

Maritime: same philosophy, three weeks earlier

Autonomous surface ships IN SERVICE SINCE 2022, CODE FROM 2026

What is autonomous
Coastal cargo vessels, ferries and inland barges under varying degrees of autonomy and remote command.
Commercial record
Yara Birkeland, built by Yara with Kongsberg Maritime, entered service in 2022 as the first fully electric autonomous container ship: 80 metres, 120 TEU, a 6,800 kWh battery, replacing about 40,000 diesel truck journeys a year on its 7-nautical-mile Norwegian route. Worth noting precisely: it began with a full crew aboard, because autonomous and uncrewed are different claims. Autoferry, New Hongzhuan, the Jindouyun vessel and the Zulu 4 barge extend the record.
Who regulates
The IMO adopted the MASS Code at MSC 111 in May 2026, effective 1 July 2026, non-mandatory for at least two years, with a mandatory version expected by 2030 via a new SOLAS chapter, weeks after UNECE adopted the road framework. Same philosophy: goal-based safety cases with in-service monitoring.
Why now
Labour supply: BIMCO and ICS projected a 26,000-officer shortfall by 2026, with officer demand up 11.8 percent since 2015.
What it teaches
IMO's four degrees of autonomy were explicitly inspired by the SAE levels used for cars. The industries are converging on shared regulatory language, a decade of separate work ending in one month of 2026.

Defence and agriculture: thinly researched here

Both belong on this page, and the coverage here is deliberately brief. Defence autonomy runs on procurement logic, classification and rules of engagement that make its safety case unlike anything else here, and treating it as simply adjacent to robotaxis would misrepresent both. Agriculture reached commercial autonomy at scale with relatively little public attention, in bounded private fields, which makes it a close second to mining as evidence for the same pattern. Both are areas I am still researching. When that work is done they will receive the same five-field treatment as the four domains above; until then, the gap is stated rather than filled.
